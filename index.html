About Me

The Real Jan

At the ripe age of , I hold a Bachelor of Science in Engineering Physics and a Master of Science in Computer Science, both from Fordham University. Along my journey, I've been exposed to multiple industries, giving me experience in tutoring, teaching, educational technology, simulation development, web development, software engineering, blog writing, and creative problem solving.

In addition to building software that helps others and creates solutions to everyday challenges, I also recognize that there's a whole world beyond the screen. During my free time, I set out on adventures that reconnect me with that world—whether it's hiking through trails, backpacking into the mountains, road cycling long distances, or embarking on multi-day bikepacking journeys.
