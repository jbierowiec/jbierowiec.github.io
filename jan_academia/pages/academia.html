Research

My research interests lie in educational development/technology, and mechatronics engineering.

My publication for a new unit of measurement for introductory physics lab, was done in collaboration with Christopher Aubin, PhD, and Jackson Saunders, BS. My work consisted of the physical development of the new units of measurement, known as "Aub sticks". The purpose of this new unit of measurment, named after the professor of this paper, was for students to find the acceleration due to gravity via blinding. Instead of using conventional meter sticks and determining that gravity is 9.81 m/s^2, here students found a new value that they didn't know if it was right or wrong. The following lab, would have the students compare all of their values to one another in order to determine if there are any outliers that need to be dropped for the final obtained gravity value in aub/s^2.

Currently, I am working on a textbook related to the most common Mathematical Proofs that all high school, and undergraduate students should know in the EM part of STEM. It starts off by introducing students to the basic mathematical properties, theorems, postulates, and then upon using these rules, certain mathematical truths are proved.
