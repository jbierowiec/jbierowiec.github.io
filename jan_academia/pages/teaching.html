Tutoring

iKlass Tutoring

At iKlass Tutoring, I have tutored students (7-12) primarily in Mathematics, specifically Algebra I, Geometry, and Algebra II. I have also prepared a few students for the SHSAT & SAT exams.

ibidPREP

At ibidPREP, I co-led a class of 8th grade students preparing for the ISEE exam for private high schools, and have done one-on-one tutoring for the SAT exam.

Bridge Golf

At Bridge Golf, I tutored students (9-12) primarily in the Mathematics & Physics subjects. For Mathematics, I have tutored students ranging from Algebra I, Geometry, Algebra, Calculus AB, and Calculus BC.
